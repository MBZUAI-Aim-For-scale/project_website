Where

The venues

MBZUAI — Masdar City

Most sessions, including the hands-on AI labs, are hosted at the Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence in Masdar City, Abu Dhabi. Registration and the opening take place here on Day 1.

UAE National Center of Meteorology (NCM)

Days 2 and 4 are hosted at NCM, the UAE's operational weather service and a WMO-affiliated regional training center, including an operational facilities tour. Buses run between the hotel and both venues — pickup times are shared each day and shown in the agenda.

What to expect

Weather in November

November is one of the most pleasant times of year in Abu Dhabi — warm, sunny days and mild evenings, with very little chance of rain. Indoor venues are air-conditioned and can feel cool.

Come prepared

What to pack

🎒 Essentials

  • Laptop & charger — required for the hands-on AI weather labs
  • Passport and any printed visa/entry documents
  • UK-style Type G plug adapter (UAE is 230V, 50Hz)
  • Any country data you've been asked to bring for the benchmarking sessions
  • Reusable water bottle

👔 Clothing

  • Smart-casual / business-casual attire for sessions
  • A light layer or jacket — venues are strongly air-conditioned
  • Comfortable shoes for the NCM facility tours
  • Modest attire is appreciated at official and cultural venues
  • Sunglasses, sunscreen, and a hat for outdoor moments (e.g. group photo, desert cultural event)
  • Light, breathable clothing for warm afternoons
Travel & entry

Getting to Abu Dhabi

Which airport should I fly into?
Abu Dhabi International Airport (AUH) is closest, about 30–40 minutes from the venues. Dubai International (DXB) is roughly 90 minutes away by road and is a common alternative.

How do I get around?
Organized buses connect the hotel with MBZUAI and NCM on session days. For personal travel, metered taxis and ride-hailing apps (Careem, Uber, and the Abu Dhabi taxi app) are widely available and affordable.
Good to know
Currency is the UAE dirham (AED); cards are accepted almost everywhere. The working week is Monday–Friday. Arabic is the official language and English is very widely spoken. The UAE is tolerant and welcoming; modest dress is appreciated at official and religious sites. Tap water is desalinated and safe, though many prefer bottled.
